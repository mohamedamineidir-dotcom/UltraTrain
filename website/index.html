Frequently asked questions

Is UltraTrain free?

Yes. You can build a training plan and start using UltraTrain for free. A premium subscription unlocks unlimited custom race plans and the full feature set.

Does it work with Apple Watch?

Yes. UltraTrain includes a standalone Apple Watch app for GPS and heart rate tracking, plus home screen widgets and Live Activities during your runs.

Is this only for trail and ultra races?

Trail and ultra distances are what UltraTrain is built for, from your first 50K to multi-day races. Road distances from 5K to marathon are supported too.

How is my finish time calculated?

UltraTrain converts your race's distance and elevation into an effort-adjusted distance, then projects your pace from your recent training and personal bests. You get three scenarios instead of one single guess.

Does UltraTrain work without an internet connection?

Yes. Your training plan, run tracking, and nutrition strategy all work fully offline, which matters when you're deep in the mountains.
